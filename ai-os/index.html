ai-os

A private tool by Giuseppe Ciccone

ai-os is a personal command-line tool that Giuseppe Ciccone uses to work with his own Google accounts — Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides — and with Google accounts he manages for clients who have authorised him to do so.

It is not a public product. Nobody else can sign up for it, and it is not offered for sale.
