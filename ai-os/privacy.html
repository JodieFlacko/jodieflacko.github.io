Privacy policy

ai-os · effective 21 October 2026

ai-os is a private tool operated by Giuseppe Ciccone (“I”). It is used only with Google accounts that I own, or that their owners have authorised me to manage.

What data it accesses

When an account owner signs in, ai-os requests access to Gmail (including its settings, such as filters), Google Calendar, Google Drive (including Drive activity), Docs, Sheets and Slides, and to the account’s basic profile (name and email address). It reads and changes this data only when I run a command that needs it — for example, reading a message, labelling a thread, creating a draft or reading a spreadsheet.

Where it is stored

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the computers I use. ai-os has no server and no database of its own. Content fetched from Google is used for the task at hand; where a task needs it, it may be saved in my own working files and their private backups.

Who it is shared with

Data is not sold, rented or used for advertising, and is not shared with third parties, with one exception: I use an AI assistant (Anthropic’s Claude) to carry out tasks, and content needed for a task I request is processed by it to complete that task.

Removing access

An account owner can revoke ai-os’s access at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions, or ask me to delete the stored tokens.
